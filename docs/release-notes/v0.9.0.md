Your countdown, reminders and day list are smarter, Glance is easier to use from the keyboard and with VoiceOver, and a round of fixes and hardening.

What's new
- A meeting about to start now takes over the countdown from a long block that is still running, such as Focus time.
- Meetings that start or end at the same time all show their countdown in the day list.
- The day list marks the same meeting as the menu bar, and a dismissed meeting loses its label.
- Today's finished meetings fold into an "N earlier" line once two or more have ended.
- Declined meetings are struck through, and tentative ones get a hollow dot.
- The reminder card's Dismiss is now "Skip this meeting". Escape closes the card without skipping.
- If an event or meeting link fails to open, Glance says so instead of doing nothing.
- About shows when Glance last checked for updates, or why it couldn't, and a new version's notes under "What's new".
- Checking for updates by hand now asks before installing, even with automatic updates on.
- Calendars and Reminders moved to the Calendar tab.
- The welcome screen shows where Glance lives and offers the countdown.

Keyboard and VoiceOver
- The month keeps keyboard and VoiceOver focus; it no longer redraws every minute.
- VoiceOver reads the menu bar item as the full date and your next meeting, and the reminder card once, when it arrives.
- Arrow keys move through the Settings sidebar and the menu bar styles, and each style now has a name.
- The reminder card works from the keyboard.
- Better contrast for Orange, Yellow and Green, and in light mode, and support for Increase Contrast.

Security
- Shortcuts that belong to macOS (such as ⌘Q or ⌘C) or to the other shortcut are refused, with a reason. Recording a shortcut pauses Glance's own.
- When you have an unanswered invitation and an accepted meeting at the same time, Join and the reminder card choose the accepted one.
- Join only opens real meeting pages on Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, Jitsi, GoTo and FaceTime.
- Windows have narrower permissions, and three unused commands are gone.

Fixes
- A broken setting no longer resets all your settings.
- Dismissed meetings stay dismissed after a restart.
- Week numbers, midnight, the hourly chime, reminders and the Dock icon behave correctly in edge cases.
- The hourly chime no longer leaks memory.
