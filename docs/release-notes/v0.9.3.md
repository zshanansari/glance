A tidier menu bar and day list, and a round of work under the hood.

Menu bar
- The text is set at the size macOS uses for the battery percentage, with digits of one width, so the countdown no longer shifts as it ticks.
- A bullet separates the icon, the date and the countdown: "• Sat Nov • in 25m".
- Far-off meetings count down in days and hours: "in 2d 5h" instead of "in 53h 46m".
- The cut-out style has more room around its date.
- New installs start with the icon alone, since the system clock already shows the date. Your own choice is kept.

Day list
- Join is now the meeting service's icon (Meet, Zoom, Teams and others), so you can see which call it opens.
- When today is empty or done, the list ends with the next meeting, such as "Next: Tomorrow, 11:45 AM". Click it to open that day.
- Event names in the menu bar can run a little longer before they are cut short.

Reminder card
- Press Return to join the meeting once the card has focus.

Fixes and under the hood
- A meeting you delegated to someone else now counts as declined, not unanswered.
- Glance reads your calendar fewer times a minute.
- The Settings style picker lines up with the rows below it.
