Add events in your own words, search your calendar, and see the days ahead.

Quick add
- Press ⌘N, or the + at the bottom of the calendar, and type something like "Lunch with Sam tomorrow 1pm at Blue Tokai". A preview shows the day, time and place before you press Return.
- "for 30m" sets the length, and a date without a time makes an all-day event.
- Undo stays next to the day for 30 seconds after an event is added.
- Choose which calendar new events go to in Settings → Events.
- Add your own Zoom, Meet or Teams link in Settings, and "on zoom" attaches it.
- With an email address, or "on meet" without a link of your own, Return opens the event in Google Calendar, which sends the invitations and makes the Meet link.

Search
- Press ⌘F, or the magnifying glass, to find events by title or place, from a month back to three months ahead. Upcoming ones come first.

Coming days
- The day list can run on into the next few days, so tomorrow's first meeting is already in view. Choose how many in Settings → Calendar.

Second time zone
- Pick a city in Settings → Calendar to see each meeting's start time there too.
- Optionally, show that city's time in the menu bar, as in "• NY 11:17 AM".

Reminders
- Snooze the meeting card for five minutes. A snooze survives a restart.
- In-person meetings can get a card 10 minutes ahead, with the place and a button that opens it in Maps.

Language and region
- Dates and the 12- or 24-hour clock follow System Settings → Language & Region.
- Week numbers can count the ISO way or the US way.

Smaller changes
- New event, search and Settings are icons at the bottom of the calendar. Point at one to see its shortcut.
- The header is back to just the month, Today and the arrows.
