The calendar no longer takes focus from the app you are in, and a round of fixes.

Calendar
- Opening the calendar, from the menu bar or with ⌃⌘K, leaves the app you were in active, with its cursor where it was. Quick add, search and the arrow keys still work in the calendar.
- A meeting you declined no longer gets the "in 15m" countdown in the day list.
- With weeks starting on Monday and US week numbers, no week is skipped.
- An all-day reminder stays on its own day when the clocks go forward.
- "Next:" gives the date for a meeting more than a week away, as in "Mon, Nov 16, 11:45 AM".

Reminder card
- A meeting you snoozed and then stopped counting down stays away.
- A snoozed meeting that moved comes back at its new time.

Updates
- An automatic update waits until the calendar, the reminder card and Settings are closed, so it never cuts off an event you are typing.

Settings
- If Glance cannot save a change, Settings says so beside the pane title.
- Changing one setting no longer resets the quick add calendar, the second time zone or a meeting link you are still typing.
- Allowing Calendar access answers at once, even while macOS is still asking about Reminders.
- Recording a new shortcut keeps the old one working until the new one is in place.

Fixes and under the hood
- Join opens Google Meet and Microsoft Teams links only when they point at a meeting.
- The app is smaller: it no longer carries its source maps or unused icons.
